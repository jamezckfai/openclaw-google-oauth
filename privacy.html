Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purpose

This application is a private, non-commercial integration connecting my personal Google account to a self-hosted AI assistant.

Information Accessed

With authorization, the application may access Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, and Google Sheets data.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide personal email management, scheduling, document access, and spreadsheet automation. Authorized information may be processed by an AI service to fulfill requested tasks.

Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are stored in protected storage on a privately managed server. Application data may be processed or stored locally as necessary to provide the integration.

Data Sharing

Google account information is not sold or used for advertising. Information may be transmitted to service providers as necessary to perform authorized tasks.

Access and Revocation

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings. Locally stored application data can be deleted by the application operator.
